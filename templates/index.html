Integrated System Architecture

From telemetry collection to AI advisory recommendations

1. IoT Monitoring

Continuous multi-parameter telemetry recording pH, Dissolved Oxygen (DO), Turbidity, TDS, Conductivity, BOD, and COD across major Ganga river basins.

2. Satellite Earth Observation

Incorporates remote sensing proxies (NDTI Normalized Difference Turbidity Index & river surface extent) for large-scale basin observation.

3. Machine Learning Pipeline

Scikit-Learn Random Forest regressor and classifier ensemble trained on historical basin data to forecast WQI 6h, 12h, 24h, and 72h ahead.

4. AI Decision Support System

Synthesizes predicted trends into actionable environmental management advisories, immediate field responses, and early warning alerts.
